About SAMIKSHAN

Born from the Development Professionals for Last-Mile Rural Transformation

SAMIKSHAN is a non-profit organization (Registered Public Trust) founded by dedicated Development Professional, with a deep commitment to promoting sustainable and inclusive development. Rather than isolated charity, we implement an Integrated Development Project model that addresses the interconnected roots of rural vulnerability.

At Present Working directly in villages across Sutahata and the wider Haldia sub-division, SAMIKSHAN strengthens grassroots institutions—empowering women through Self-Help Groups (SHGs), expanding remedial education and vocational skills for youth, organizing preventive healthcare and mental health awareness camps, and partnering with Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)and The Neotia University to equip smallholder farmers with modern, organic agricultural practices and direct market linkages.

Child Education & Skills

Foundational literacy, school retention support, and market-relevant vocational training.

Community & Maternal Health

Rural health camps, maternal-child nutrition, and stigma-free mental health awareness.

Community Institution Building

Federating women’s Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and village development committees.

Livelihood Facilitation

KVK-guided organic farming, micro-enterprises, and collective market linkages.

Five Thematic Pillars of Grassroots Action

Schedule VII (ii)

Education & Skill Development

Bridging learning gaps for first-generation learners in rural Haldia through accessible child education centers, remedial coaching, and job-oriented vocational training for rural youth.

  • Community remedial learning & digital literacy hubs
  • School retention drives & learning kit distribution
  • Vocational tailoring, handicrafts & trade skill workshops

Schedule VII (i)

Healthcare & Well-being

Delivering preventive healthcare to underserved hamlets through mobile medical camps, maternal and child nutrition programs, and community mental health sensitization.

  • Multi-specialty rural health check-up & eye screening camps
  • Antenatal care, anemia reduction & child immunization awareness
  • Community mental health awareness & counseling workshops
Target: Mothers, Infants & Elderly Support Program →
KVK Partnered

Sustainable Agriculture (KVK)

In collaboration with Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), SAMIKSHAN trains smallholder farmers in climate-resilient, organic farming methods and builds direct market linkages.

  • KVK-led training on bio-fertilizers, vermicomposting & soil health
  • Household nutrition gardens for year-round dietary diversity
  • Collective aggregation & fair-price local market linkages
Schedule VII (iv) & (x) Support Program →
Schedule VII (iii)

Women’s Empowerment & SHGs

Strengthening grassroots women’s Self-Help Groups (SHGs) through financial literacy, thrift-and-credit governance, and seed support for women-led rural micro-enterprises.

  • SHG bookkeeping, digital banking & financial literacy training
  • Enterprise incubation in food processing, poultry & tailoring
  • Women’s leadership & civic participation workshops
Target: Rural Women & SHGs Support Program →
Schedule VII (iv)

Environmental Sustainability

Protecting the coastal-deltaic ecology of Purba Medinipur via community plantation drives, pond and waterbody rejuvenation, and village-level solid waste management.

  • Native & fruit-bearing tree plantation with survival tracking
  • Source segregation, plastic-free village & composting drives
  • School eco-clubs & cyclone-resilience awareness
Target: Haldia & Sutahata Belt Support Program →
